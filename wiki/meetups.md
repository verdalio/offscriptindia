meetups here start small and informal. a few ground rules keep them good for everyone:

- meet in public places first: cafes, parks, busy spots. never a private home for a first meetup.
- tell a friend where you are going and when you expect to be back.
- organize in the open, in post comments, so everyone can see the plan.
- no entry fees collected over dm. if money is ever involved (splitting a bill, booking a court), settle it in person.
- anyone making you uncomfortable? report the post or message the mods.

if a city gets regulars, we can spin up a recurring meetup thread for it.
