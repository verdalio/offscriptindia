# community setup spec

subreddit: r/OffScriptIndia (public, sfw, topic: q&as & stories)
created: november 12, 2026

## tagline

Off the default script. Post anything, all of India welcome.

## sidebar description

A chill India wide hangout for life off the default script. Post anything: things you are building, running and sports, bike rides, travel life, friendships and dating, relationship stuff, childfree or marriagefree takes, work wins. Solo, partnered, open, still figuring it out, all welcome. No convincing anybody, no entry test. If it grows, OptOut Club meetups can start in cities and towns across India.

## rules

1. Be kind about every path. Every life setup is welcome here.
2. No convincing. Share your life, don't recruit or judge.
3. No bashing. Disagree kindly about people and choices.
4. Keep it chill. No hate, no spam, no selling, no personals.
5. Protect privacy. No doxxing, no screenshots of private chats.
6. Meet safely. Public places first, tell a friend.

## post flair

every post needs a topic flair. add a city flair when the post is about a specific place.

topic flairs (yellow #F2B33D): Hangout, Meetup, Story, Ask, Win, Rant (kind)
topic flairs (navy #1E1B2E, light text): Building, Running + Sports, Bike Rides, Travel Life, Relationships
city flairs (blue-grey #8FB8BF): Pune, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Small town
