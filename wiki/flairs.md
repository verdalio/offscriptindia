every post needs a topic flair. add a city flair when your post is about a specific place.

topic flairs (yellow): hangout, meetup, story, ask, win, rant (kind)
topic flairs (navy): building, running + sports, bike rides, travel life, relationships
city flairs (blue): pune, mumbai, delhi ncr, bengaluru, hyderabad, chennai, kolkata, ahmedabad, small town

how to add flair: on the create post page, tap the flair option under the title and pick one of each.
